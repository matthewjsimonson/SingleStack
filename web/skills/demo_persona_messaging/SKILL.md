# Persona messaging

Tune the message to a specific buyer: the outcome they own, the objection that stops them, and the words they actually use.

## When to use
When messaging reads one-size-fits-all, when entering a new persona or segment, or when buyer-intent signals reveal language or objections the current copy misses.

## Inputs
- The GTM record's personas/ICP, industries, and value prop.
- Buyer-intent and review signals (e.g. G2), win themes, and objections from real engagements.

## Process
1. **Lead with their outcome.** Open with what *this* persona is accountable for — a product leader, an economic buyer, and an end user each need a different first line.
2. **Address the top objection.** Name the one thing that would stop them ("just an AI wrapper?", "governance/audit?") and answer it up front.
3. **Use their language.** Pull phrasing from real signals rather than inventing it; cite the signal.
4. **One ask.** End on the single next step that fits where they are in the journey.

## Principles
- Outcome before features, always.
- Language comes from evidence (real buyer words), not imagination.
- One persona, one objection, one ask per message — resist the kitchen sink.

## Output
Per persona: a one-line hook tied to their outcome, the top objection handled, language drawn from cited signals, and one clear CTA. No generic "AI for everyone" copy. Proposed for ratification.
