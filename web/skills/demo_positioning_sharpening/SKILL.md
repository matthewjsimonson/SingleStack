# Positioning sharpening

Make how the product is positioned specific, defensible, and impossible to confuse with the alternatives a buyer is weighing.

## When to use
When positioning reads generic, overlaps a competitor, leans on hype, or hasn't been re-grounded against recent market and competitive signals.

## Inputs
- The GTM record's positioning, category POV, and differentiation fields.
- Competitive signals and the capability matrix (who the real alternatives are).
- Market signals and buyer-intent language.

## Process
1. **Name the category we're reframing.** Lead with the shift — a living system of record for product + GTM, where agents propose and humans ratify.
2. **Name what we are NOT.** Contrast explicitly with the alternatives by name (roadmapping tools, CI feeds, call analytics). The reader should know in one line why we're a different kind of thing.
3. **Ground every claim in a signal.** Each positioning statement traces to a signal or theme; if it can't, mark it a hypothesis.
4. **Cut hype.** Replace adjectives with proof — "AI-powered" becomes the specific capability and the evidence it works.

## Principles
- Differentiation is only real if a buyer could restate it — test every line against "could a stranger repeat this accurately?"
- No claim without evidence; a hypothesis is labeled, not asserted.
- Reframe weaknesses honestly; don't bury them.

## Output
A positioning line a stranger could repeat after reading once: the category, the wedge, one concrete proof — no overlap with the alternatives, nothing unsupported. Proposed for ratification.
